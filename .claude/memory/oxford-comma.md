Always use the Oxford comma: "rate, rights, and disclosure," never "rate, rights and disclosure."

**Why:** The user's house style; they called out its absence on the brief deck ("We always use Oxford commas. Come on.").

**How to apply:** Every series of three or more, in slides, docs, notes, commit messages, and UI copy. Check new writing before showing it. Also recorded in AGENTS.md and design-system/05-content/voice-and-tone.md.
