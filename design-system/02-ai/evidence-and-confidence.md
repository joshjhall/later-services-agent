# Evidence and confidence

**Status:** Draft

The agent shows **why**, one criterion at a time, so the CM can check the reasoning in seconds (HAX G11; recruiting tools' "met / partly / not met").

- Each row is `label → value`, with a tone: good (`success`), bad (`destructive`) or neutral.
- Values are concrete and checkable: "90% on time (9 of 10)", not "reliable".
- Confidence is a word (high / medium / low) tied to the evidence, not a decimal. Show what would change it when it's low.
- Never present a single composite score as the reason for a decision.
